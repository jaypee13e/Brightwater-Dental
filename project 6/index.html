Common questions

Do you see children?

Yes. We welcome children from their first tooth, and we keep visits short and friendly.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Book an emergency visit online, or call us. We keep slots free each day for pain, chips and lost fillings.

Can I cancel or change my appointment?

Yes. Just let us know at least 24 hours ahead and we’ll move it at no charge.
